Parent Reference Guide

Clinic Sick Visit vs. Emergency Room

Quick decision guide for parents when a child experiences unexpected symptoms.

Symptom Category LittleSteps Same-Day Clinic Emergency Room (Call 911)
Fever Fever lasting >48 hours, mild lethargy in older child Fever in infant under 3 months, or fever with stiff neck
Breathing Mild cough, runny nose, mild wheezing Severe wheezing, rib retracting, gasping for air
Injuries Minor scrapes, small cuts, mild sprains Deep wounds, suspected bone fractures, head trauma
Stomach Mild vomiting or diarrhea with fluid tolerance Severe abdominal pain, signs of severe dehydration
