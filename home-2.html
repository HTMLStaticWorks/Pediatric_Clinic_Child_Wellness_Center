Parent Emergency Guide

Where should you go when your child is unwell?

Quick clinical decision guide to help parents choose the right care channel.

📞 24/7 Nurse Triage

Call Nurse Line First

For mild fever under 48 hours, mild cough, feeding questions, minor rashes, or medication dosage advice.

Call (555) 234-5678
🏥 Same-Day Clinic

Book Clinic Visit

For earache, sore throat, persistent fever, vomiting, minor cuts, asthma flare-ups, or suspected sprains.

Book Same-Day Visit
🚨 Emergency Room / 911

Go to Nearest ER

For severe shortness of breath, fever in baby under 3 months, seizures, head trauma, or unresponsiveness.

Call 911 Immediately
