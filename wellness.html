Growth & Milestones

Key developmental stages for every age.

Our wellness team tracks and supports every milestone as your child grows.

0–12 Months

Infant Wellness

Focus on motor development, feeding progression, sensory response and gentle sleep habits.

1–3 Years

Toddler Growth

Encouraging language expansion, positive mealtime habits, physical play and social discovery.

3–5 Years

Preschool Readiness

Fine motor coordination, social interactions, emotional awareness and active daily play.

6–12 Years

School Age Health

Support for physical fitness, balanced screen habits, posture, sleep hygiene and academic focus.
